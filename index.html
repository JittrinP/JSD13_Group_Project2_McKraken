Care Instructions

Keep Them Blooming

Our floral arrangements are designed to last. Follow these simple steps to ensure your blooms stay vibrant and fresh for as long as possible.

  • Trim the Stems Cut 1 inch off the bottom at a 45-degree angle before placing in water.
  • Fresh Water Daily Change the water every 2 days and clean the vase to prevent bacteria.
